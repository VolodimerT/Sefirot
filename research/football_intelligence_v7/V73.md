## Score interpretation

Football Intelligence measures coverage of five predeclared dimensions, 20 points each. These are calendar freshness, venue-specific history for both teams, venue-specific xG for both teams, verified lineups and assigned referee. Data Confidence covers the three target-specific checks (calendar freshness, lineups, referee), one third each. Equal weights reflect a count of satisfied prerequisites, with no empirical preference established. Missing evidence earns zero readiness credit and remains explicit in the checklist. Neither percentage is calibrated confidence in the predicted outcome.

Model Reliability requires independent paired calibration evidence. Market Fit requires a supported contract and a testable tactical premise. Price Evidence requires a dated, admissible quote for the exact contract. With insufficient evidence these fields are null. Risk reports team sample sizes and calibrated interval availability. Consensus is undefined without comparable independently recorded opinions on a common hypothesis. Do not sum these axes. Risk and price remain in their native units. PASS retains the score scenarios and sports probabilities.

For an original seal, score_review.score_original delegates to arena_diagnostic's diagnose, including the existing control binding and role-specific blinded views. Role scores measure the percentage of findings with a known state and attributable evidence. Counterexamples, test conditions and unknowns remain attached. Source lists in unsealed daily dossiers are work assignments only. The old forward scorecard scores original decisions only. The verified research export has zero registered predictions. There is no paired old score or prospective veto to grade. Postmatch rows preserve the evidence checklist, proper scores, tactical testability and evidence-based decision review separately.
